# Yam Olisker: The Tech Hype Simulator

## 1. Core Gameplay Loop (The Daily Grind)

### Overview Loop
Every in-game day is a high-pressure loop:
1. **Scan rumors and market signals** → 2. **Pick content angle** → 3. **Film performance-heavy footage** → 4. **Edit at speed** → 5. **Publish and manage community** → 6. **Evaluate metrics and recover before next cycle**.

The player’s score each day is driven by four live KPIs:
- **Views Velocity** (first 60 minutes performance)
- **Audience Trust** (long-term credibility)
- **Hype Momentum** (short-term excitement)
- **Creator Stability** (burnout resistance)

### Research Phase: Leak Hunting & Rumor Triage
The day opens in a “War Room” dashboard styled like a chaotic multi-monitor setup.

**Systems and choices:**
- **Intel Sources:** Public X posts, private Discord channels, niche forums, supply-chain chatter, and “dark web leak boards.”
- **Credibility Heatmap:** Each source has hidden reliability + motive tags (clout chasing, insider, satirical, competitor bait).
- **Rumor Scoring:** Player chooses 1–3 rumors to pursue based on:
  - potential hype impact,
  - risk of being fake,
  - time sensitivity,
  - sponsor alignment.
- **Investigative Actions:** DM leakers, cross-check serial numbers, analyze blurry photos, call industry contacts.

**Mini-outcomes:**
- **“Early and Right”** gives a Hype surge + Trust boost.
- **“Early and Wrong”** gives instant views but long Trust damage.
- **“Late but Correct”** gives lower peak views but stable audience confidence.

### Filming Mechanic: Performance vs Accuracy
Filming is a real-time mini-game played across scene cards: intro hook, hands-on demo, opinion rant, and CTA.

**Core meters:**
- **Energy Meter (Enthusiasm):** fuels expressive delivery, comedic timing, and viral moments.
- **Accuracy Meter (Technical Correctness):** tracks factual precision and nuance.
- **Composure Meter:** affected by caffeine, sleep, and recent stress.

**Gameplay actions:**
- Hit timed prompts for voice emphasis, facial expression, hand gestures, and camera movement.
- Trigger the **“Shocked Thumbnail Window”** at the right frame for max click-through potential.
- Choose sentence variants (hype-heavy vs technical) while speaking.

**Risk/reward:**
- High enthusiasm + low accuracy = viral spike, comment wars, trust erosion.
- High accuracy + flat delivery = critical praise, weak algorithm pickup.
- Perfect take = CTR bonus, retention bonus, and positive memeability.

### Editing Mode: Rhythm Jump-Cut System
Editing is a lane-based rhythm game synchronized to soundtrack BPM and speech waveform.

**Mechanics:**
- **Cut-on-beat inputs** for punchy jump edits.
- **Overlay timing** for text callouts, specs popups, and zoom punches.
- **B-roll stitching** windows for product beauty shots.
- **Audio ducking** prompts when sound effects overlap narration.

**Failure states:**
- Missed beats create **awkward silence gaps** and retention drops.
- Overediting causes “viewer fatigue” debuff.
- Sloppy sync triggers “Amateur Cut” comments reducing perceived quality.

**Mastery bonuses:**
- Consistent combo grants **Flow State**, briefly slowing time for precision edits.
- Stylish streak unlocks signature transitions and “Yam Cut Packs.”

### Publishing & Community Management
Publishing triggers a live simulation of the first 24-hour performance curve.

**Launch decisions:**
- Title style (bold claim vs investigative framing)
- Thumbnail intensity (reaction face, product close-up, leak circle annotation)
- Upload timing by region
- Notification strategy (short teaser, pinned community post)

**Comment Section Command Center:**
- **Reply to fans:** grows loyalty and parasocial bond.
- **Ignore trolls:** preserves energy but can allow negativity spirals.
- **Pin strategic comments:** steers narrative, increases watch-time chains.
- **Moderate misinformation:** protects trust at mental-energy cost.

**Community archetypes:**
- Core fans, spec nerds, brand warriors, casual drama seekers, and hate-watchers.
Each reacts differently to your tone and accuracy.

## 2. The "Yam Details" (Specific Mechanics)

### The Hype Meter (Primary Meta System)
A global meter from 0–100 representing channel hype intensity.

**Low Hype (<30):**
- Flat recommendations, sponsor disinterest, stale comments.
- Recovery requires fresh formats, exclusive access, or high-trust reporting.

**Optimal Hype (40–75):**
- Strong growth and balanced stability.
- Best zone for sustainable scaling.

**Overhype (76–100): Burnout Risk Zone**
- Massive short-term gains.
- Rising stress events: insomnia, creative fatigue, voice strain, sloppy research.
- Can trigger **Burnout Mode** (temporary stat collapse and forced recovery arc).

### Coffee & Energy System
Caffeine is a tactical resource, not a free buff.

**Stats affected:**
- Reaction speed (filming + editing inputs)
- Speech rate and enthusiasm
- Hand steadiness during macro b-roll
- Anxiety and sleep quality

**Dose curve:**
- 1 espresso: focus boost.
- 2–3: max productivity window.
- 4+: shaky camera, rushed speech, increased error chance.
- 6+: “Overclocked Brain” event (hallucinated notifications, missed facts).

Players can offset crash via hydration, meals, naps, and breathing routines, but this costs precious timeline hours.

### Gear Acquisition Syndrome (G.A.S)
Progression from bedroom creator to global studio mogul.

**Tier progression:**
1. **Starter Setup:** webcam, ring light, entry mic, noisy room.
2. **Creator Upgrade:** mirrorless camera, sound treatment, LED panels.
3. **Pro Lab:** cinema cameras, robot sliders, premium lenses, multi-cam control.
4. **Empire Studio:** RED rigs, modular sets, motion walls, custom light grid, showcase vault.

**System depth:**
- Gear provides real stat changes (image clarity, setup speed, creative options).
- Excessive buying can drain cashflow and increase clutter penalties.
- Unopened gadget shelves give cosmetic prestige but can trigger “consumerism backlash” comments.

### The Unboxing Race (Flagship Timed Mission)
A global event mission when major devices launch.

**Mission phases:**
1. **Secure Unit:** negotiate with PR, retailers, scalpers, courier hacks.
2. **Transit Rush:** traffic, customs, battery issues, lost luggage hazards.
3. **Rapid Setup:** set lighting, activate scripts, calibrate camera profile.
4. **Record & Edit Sprint:** reduced margin for mistakes.
5. **First-to-publish race:** each minute late reduces projected view spike.

**Dynamic rivals:** AI competitors with distinct behavior (reckless clickbaiter, ultra-technical reviewer, entertainment giant).

## 4. Challenges & Random Events

### "The Leak Was Fake"
You posted based on unreliable intel.

**Immediate effects:** trust drop, backlash spike, clip compilations mocking your take.

**Player response options:**
- Rapid correction video (transparency bonus)
- Quiet edit + no mention (short-term mitigation, long-term trust damage)
- Blame source (can preserve image or look evasive)

### "Tech Failure"
Example: Vision Pro crashes mid-review.

**Outcomes depend on handling:**
- Rage reaction boosts entertainment value.
- Calm troubleshooting boosts expert credibility.
- Repeated failures can trigger sponsor concern.

### "Algorithm Change"
YouTube shifts recommendation weights overnight.

**Simulation impact:**
- 50% view drop for old format.
- New hidden preference model favors different pacing/topic mix.

**Adaptation toolkit:**
- A/B thumbnail labs
- format pivot experiments
- short-form funnel strategy
- community challenge events to revive interaction

### Additional Chaos Events (Optional Pool)
- Sponsor demands script control.
- Rival leaks your private production budget.
- Internet outage during upload deadline.
- Mic battery dies during once-in-a-lifetime interview.
- Viral clip taken out of context triggers brand war.

### Long-Term Progression
- Grow from niche local creator to multilingual global tech authority.
- Unlock teams (research assistant, editor, producer, community manager).
- Build reputation tracks: **Entertainer**, **Analyst**, **Insider**, or hybrid archetypes.

### Win Conditions (Sandbox + Campaign)
**Campaign victory:**
- Reach global authority tier,
- maintain high trust for a full season,
- launch a flagship studio event with record-breaking engagement,
- avoid permanent burnout collapse.

**Sandbox mode:**
- Endless content cycles with dynamic tech eras and shifting platform rules.

### Endgame Tension
The ultimate challenge is not just maximizing views—it is balancing:
- Hype without self-destruction,
- speed without misinformation,
- growth without losing identity.

That tension is the core fantasy of **Yam Olisker: The Tech Hype Simulator**.
